1. What have we already covered?
   - Variables, types, collections, indexing, mutation, loops, conditions, functions, scope, arguments, modules, imports and basic packaging.
2. What important Python topics remain?
   - Classes and objects (Day 16), files (Day 24), comprehensions (Day 26), exceptions (Day 30), and more advanced iteration.
3. What should we prioritise?
   - First, strengthen your understanding of Python itself. Then NumPy, pandas, testing, APIs and databases.
4. What should we de-emphasise from the course?
   - Turtle graphics, arcade games and extensive GUI development. They're useful exercises, but less relevant to your statistical programming goals.
We'll also cover a few things that the course doesn't emphasise enough for your purposes, particularly Python's object model, testing with pytest, and writing maintainable data-processing code.
